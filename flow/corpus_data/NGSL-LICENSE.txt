NAWL and BSL word lists (NGSL Project) - attribution and licence

Flow's word books 学术英语 (book "nawl") and 商务英语 (book "bsl") in corpus_data/wordbooks-open.json
are adapted from two word lists of the NGSL Project (https://www.newgeneralservicelist.com/).

1. New Academic Word List (NAWL), version 1.2
   "New Academic Word List by Browne, C., Culligan, B., and Phillips, J. is licensed under a
   Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 4.0 International License."
   Source: https://www.newgeneralservicelist.com/new-academic-word-list
   File used: "NAWL 1.2 alphabetized" (NAWL_1.2_alphabetized_description.txt, 957 headwords)
   Citation suggested by the authors: Browne, C., Culligan, B. (2013). The New Academic Word List.
   Retrieved from http://www.newgeneralservicelist.com

2. Business Service List (BSL), version 1.2 (files named 1.20)
   "Business Service List by Browne, C. and Culligan, B., is licensed under a Creative Commons
   Attribution-ShareAlike 4.0 International License."
   Source: https://www.newgeneralservicelist.com/business-service-list
   File used: "BSL 1.2 list with basic statistics" (BSL_1.20_stats.csv, 1744 headwords with BSL Rank)
   Citation suggested by the authors: Browne, C., Culligan, B. (2013). The Business Service List.
   Retrieved from http://www.newgeneralservicelist.com

Changes made by Flow (tools/build_open_wordbooks.py; details in flow/WORDBOOKS-OPEN.md):
   - headwords lower-cased and matched to entries of Flow's dictionary; a few forms mapped to the
     dictionary entry that carries the listed sense (onwards -> onward, headquarter -> headquarters,
     unauthorize -> unauthorized);
   - removed: prefixes the lists carry as items (anti, ex, micro, multi, neo, non, pre, semi, sub,
     trans) and words Flow's dictionary has no Chinese meaning for (for example goods, tranche);
   - NAWL put in word-frequency order (the NAWL 1.2 file is alphabetical); BSL keeps its BSL Rank order;
   - Chinese meanings shown with the words come from ECDICT (MIT, corpus_data/ECDICT-LICENSE.txt), not
     from the NGSL Project.
